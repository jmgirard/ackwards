# M90: Oblique rotation as a documented non-default option

**Status:** done (2026-09-30, PR #99 https://github.com/jmgirard/ackwards/pull/99)

**Goal:** Offer oblique rotation as a documented, non-default `rotation` argument on every engine, with real within-level factor correlations, correlation-preserving oblique scoring, oblique-valid variance, a loud fit-time advisory, and a Forbes oracle, while default varimax output stays unchanged.

**Outcome:** `ackwards()` gains `rotation = "varimax"`, which also accepts `"oblimin"` and `"promax"` (PCA, EFA through psych) and `"oblimin"` and `"geomin"` (ESEM through lavaan). `.check_rotation()` and `.supported_rotations` check each engine's rotations and run `rlang::check_installed("GPArotation")` on the psych paths that load it (D-037). `boot_edges()` refits with the object's rotation and runs the same check. `.tenBerge_weights(R, L, Phi)` takes Φ (ten Berge 1999 Eq. 9, `L* = LΦ^{1/2}`). The fallback is psych's R⁻¹LΦ weights for EFA and `.regression_weights()` for ESEM. `.variance_key()` and `.variance_explained()` use `diag(ΦΛ'Λ)`, which also drives the ESEM sort. `.engine_phi()` and `.esem_read_phi()` (matched by lavaan factor name) read Φ, and a non-finite Φ truncates the level. A failed rotation follows the start count. `psych::pca()` rotates once, so its rotation warnings and errors truncate. `psych::fa()` (start count from `.psych_fa_starts()`) and lavaan (warnings turned on by `.esem_rotation_args()`) warn and keep the level. An EFA level with no `rot.mat` still truncates. Per D-036, `r` drives lineage. The fit-time advisory, `print()` and `summary()` notes, the `autoplot()` caption, and a `prune()` warning naming its rules say so. Fixture `forbes2023_oblique.rds` (OSF `7jfkw`, md5-pinned `data-raw/forbes2023-oblique.R`, GPArotation 2026.6-1 or later), oracles O16 to O19, and an invariance test (LΦL' equals the varimax LL'). GPArotation entered Suggests (D-035). Docs: `?ackwards`, the engines vignette's rotation section, NEWS, DESIGN §4, §7, §9 and Known limitations, the intro vignette, and the README.

**Decisions:** D-036 (the owner settled BC5 and BC6 without the Forbes session: `r` drives lineage, and `prune()` warns) and D-037 (the GPArotation guard covers only the paths that load it). Milestone-local: the rotation-failure rule by start count, corrected once at the implement gate after `psych::fa()` turned out to use 20 starts. PCA and EFA keep psych's column order. Fidelity tolerance is 1e-4 for minres with oblimin (psych's random starts) and 1e-10 elsewhere, with edges compared through her D standardization.

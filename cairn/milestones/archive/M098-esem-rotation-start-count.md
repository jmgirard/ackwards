# M098: Counted lavaan rotation failures on every ESEM rotation

**Status:** done (2026-10-06, PR #110 https://github.com/jmgirard/ackwards/pull/110)

**Goal:** ESEM counts lavaan's failed rotation starts under every rotation, varimax included, and ends the hierarchy at a level where every start failed.

**Outcome:** `.esem_rotation_args()` turns lavaan's rotation warnings on for varimax as well as oblimin and geomin. k = 1 stays unrotated. `.esem_fit_one()` counts the "rotation algorithm did not converge" warnings, after collapsing whitespace, and reads the start total through `.esem_rotation_starts()`. That helper returns `rotation.args$rstarts`, or 1 when the value is 0 or cannot be read. When some starts failed, the level is kept and the assembly loop warns "<n> of <N> random starts did not converge". When every start failed, the level returns `nonconverged` and the hierarchy ends at the level before, with a warning that the kept rotation did not converge. `lavInspect(fit, "converged")` describes only the model fit. New `tests/testthat/test-esem-rotation-starts.R` has a deterministic partial plant (varimax and oblimin) and real `max_iter = 2` plants (varimax, oblimin, and geomin on ML, varimax on WLSMV). It also checks that two default fits raise no warning, and it unit-tests the helper. M90's oblique-only test was replaced, and `.warnings_of()` moved to `helper-data.R`. Docs: the `?ackwards` rotation section, NEWS (M90's bullet corrected too), and DESIGN §4.

**Decisions:** milestone-local. The rule replaces M90's keep-always rule and its silent varimax, at the user's plan-gate choice. A default varimax fit changes only when all 30 starts fail, and none of the measured fits did. D-034 is not superseded. A T1 measurement on lavaan 0.7.2 gave exactly 30 of 30 warnings for 3 rotations and 5 estimator paths.

**Review:** one pass, and all six criteria passed. Three lenses gave 23 findings. Nine were fixed before merge: whitespace-safe matching, a tested `rstarts` fallback, wording for each rotated level, M90's NEWS bullet, the k = 2 silence check, and the Decisions entry. Two went to candidate rows (pre-0.7 lavaan count, failed-start count on the object), and twelve were rejected. Merged on local green under the repo's standing non-release rule. One LESSONS line was retired by enforcement (M90's lavaan rotation warnings are off by default, now failed by the all-fail plants), and one was added.
